Teach the classifier rules only

Record yourself doing each behavior (and "normal" sitting) a few times. Only pose numbers are stored, never video. Then press Train: a classifier is fitted to your examples in the browser and runs alongside the rules. More examples, more people, more angles = better model. Export the set to train the backend with scripts/train_behavior.py.
How this works
  1. Each camera is a room. Frames go to MoveNet MultiPose (TensorFlow.js) running in your browser: up to 6 people, 17 keypoints each, with a built-in box tracker giving stable ids. In the Python backend this stage is YOLOv8-pose + ByteTrack on an RTSP feed; the classifiers downstream are the same.
  2. A second model, MediaPipe Face Landmarker, gives lip movement (jaw-open blendshape) for the talking cue. That only resolves at short range; in a classroom this cue needs an audio channel.
  3. Per track, each frame yields cues: torso motion, head motion, a nearby moving partner, relative velocity, sustained arm raise, head down (nose below shoulders, or the head dropping versus this person's own upright baseline learned in the first seconds, or the head tilted onto a hand), foot point outside every seat zone, lip movement, head turning.
  4. Each behavior sits behind a sustained-condition gate with a minimum duration and a minimum fraction of active frames. Demo timing: fighting 2 s, sleeping 6 s, out of seat 4 s, talking 3 s. Classroom timing: 3 / 20 / 8 / 5 s. A single frame can never alert.
  5. A seat zone is created automatically around anyone who stays put for a few seconds without one (labelled "auto seat"); you can also draw zones by hand.
  6. Repeats merge into the open alert; per-track (120 s) and per-room (30 s) cooldowns stop duplicates.
  7. On alert a clip is kept from a 10 s pre-roll ring buffer plus 10 s post-roll. Nothing is recorded continuously.
  8. A staff member must acknowledge then confirm or dismiss. Only a human confirm marks a clip actionable; the system takes no action itself. Retention: unreviewed 48 h, dismissed 24 h, confirmed 90 d.
  • Video never leaves the device. There is no server; clips live in page memory and vanish on reload.
  • Talking is on but deliberately weak. Nothing at this distance resolves lip motion, so the only cue is repeated head turning; events are hard-capped at LOW confidence, can never reach HIGH, and are a nudge to look, not evidence. In a classroom the rule also requires a neighbour within reach; that requirement is relaxed here so one person testing alone can see it. Add a per-room audio level channel before trusting it.
  • Seat zones are saved per camera in this browser.

Alert queue

Alerts are suggestions from an automated detector. A staff member must acknowledge and review each one; nothing is flagged actionable until a person confirms it.
