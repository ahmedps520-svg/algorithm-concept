How this demo works
  1. Each room is a simulated 720p camera feed rendered in the browser. Boxes and track ids are what the detector + tracker (YOLOv8-pose + ByteTrack in the real backend) would output.
  2. Every track feeds per-frame cues (motion, proximity, relative velocity, arm raise, head-below-shoulders, foot outside seat zone) into a sustained-condition gate: a behavior fires only after it has held for several seconds at a high fraction of frames. A single frame can never alert.
  3. The alert engine merges repeats into the open alert and applies per-track (120 s) and per-room (30 s) cooldowns.
  4. On alert, a clip is saved from a 10 s pre-roll ring buffer plus 10 s post-roll. Nothing is recorded continuously.
  5. A staff member must acknowledge then confirm or dismiss. Only a human confirm marks a clip actionable. The system never takes any action itself.
  6. Retention is driven by the human outcome: unreviewed clips are deleted after 48 h, dismissed after 24 h, confirmed kept 90 days.
Same UI, thresholds and workflow as the Python backend in this repository; the video and detections here are synthetic. Talking detection is off: from video alone at this distance it is low confidence and needs an audio channel.
